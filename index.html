Talya Muse Connector

Talya Muse Connector is a personal, single-user integration. It connects the owner's own Pinterest account to the owner's personal AI assistant, Muse, through the official Pinterest API.

With the owner's authorization, the connector can:

  • Read the owner's Pinterest profile, boards and Pins
  • Create boards and Pins on the owner's behalf
  • Help schedule, organize and report on the owner's own content

The connector is used by one person only, for personal use. It is not offered to the public, it does not act on other people's Pinterest accounts, and it does not sell or share data.

Owner's Pinterest profile: pinterest.com/talya
