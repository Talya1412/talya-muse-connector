Privacy Policy: Talya Muse Connector

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Who operates this app

Talya Muse Connector is a personal, single-user integration operated by the owner of the Pinterest account talya. It connects that account, and only that account, to the owner's personal AI assistant, Muse, through the official Pinterest API.

What data the app accesses

After the owner signs in with Pinterest and approves the requested permissions, the app can access the owner's own Pinterest data within these scopes: user_accounts:read, boards:read, boards:write, pins:read and pins:write. This means the owner's profile details, boards and Pins.

How the data is used

The data is used only to manage the owner's own Pinterest account at the owner's direction: viewing profile, boards and Pins, creating boards and Pins, and organizing or reporting on the owner's own content. The data is not sold, not rented, not shared with advertisers, not used for third-party analytics, and not used to act on anyone else's account.

Storage and retention

Access is held as OAuth tokens issued by Pinterest. Tokens are stored in the secure credential storage of the Muse platform, never in this website's code or in public files. Pinterest access tokens expire after 30 days and refresh tokens after 60 days unless refreshed. Pinterest content retrieved through the API is processed to complete the owner's requested task and is not kept as a separate public dataset.

Sharing

No Pinterest data obtained through this app is disclosed to third parties, except the services needed to run the integration itself: Pinterest (the API provider) and the Muse platform (which stores the OAuth credential securely and makes the API calls on the owner's behalf).

Revoking access

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time in Pinterest account settings, under Apps or authorized app permissions, or by disconnecting the connector in Muse. Revoking access stops all further API calls.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the owner's Pinterest profile: pinterest.com/talya.
